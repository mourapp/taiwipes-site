How it works

1. Pop the lid

Open the flip-top and pull out one soft wipe.

2. Wipe

Gently wipe your teeth and around your mouth for a clean, fresh feel.

3. Toss it

Throw the wipe in the trash when you are done. Single use. Please do not flush.
