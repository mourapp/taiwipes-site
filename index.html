Tai Wipes are oral mouth wipes that come in one resealable pack with many soft wipes inside. Pop the lid, take one out, and freshen up after coffee, after a meal, on a plane, or before you walk into the room.

One pack. Many wipes. Zero fuss.

Tai Wipes is a whole unit — a soft pack with a flip-top lid that keeps every wipe moist and ready. It is not a finger wipe, and it is not a single wipe sealed alone in a packet. You carry the pack, and the pack carries you through the day: at your desk, in your bag, in the car, at the gym.

How it works

1. Pop the lid

Open the flip-top and pull out one soft wipe.

2. Wipe

Gently wipe your teeth and around your mouth for a clean, fresh feel.

3. Toss it

Throw the wipe in the trash when you are done. Single use. Please do not flush.
